---
title: Due and planned dates
description: The difference between a task's due date and its planned date, how to set each, and how Today, Upcoming, the calendar, the timeline and the auto-planner use them.
---

A task has two dates, and they answer different questions:

| Date | Question | Time of day |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Due date** | By when must it be finished? This is the deadline. | Optional, such as 17:00 |
| **Planned date** | On which day will you work on it? | Yes, optionally: **Start** in the details panel, or a drag onto an hour in the week view. The task is then a block from that time, as long as its estimate, until its **Finish** |

Both are optional and independent. A task can have a deadline next Friday and be planned for Wednesday. Set a due date only for real deadlines, and use the planned date to spread the work over the week.

## Set the dates

| Where | How |
| --- | --- |
| Quick add | A date typed in the title, such as *friday 10:00* or *next monday*, becomes the **due date**. The **Plan for a day** chip sets the planned date. A click on a day in the calendar or on the timeline opens quick add with that day already planned. |
| Details panel | **Due date** (*Add a deadline*) and **Start** (*Pick when to work on it*), a day with an optional time; with a time, **Finish** sets when the block ends. |
| Calendar, month view | Drag a task to another day. Drop it on the **Due** flag to make that day its deadline. |
| Timeline | Drag a bar's start edge for the planned date, its end edge for the due date, or the whole bar to move both. A bar without a deadline also gets one, at its end, when its start edge is dragged. |
| Agents | `due` and `planned` in `create_task` and `update_task`, or a whole day with `reschedule_day`. |

The date picker has three ways to choose a day:

- **Type a date**: phrases like *fri*, *tomorrow 15:00*, *in 2 weeks* or *oct 15*. The picker shows the date it read; **Enter** takes it. A time on its own (*2pm*) keeps the day already chosen.
- Suggestions: **Today**, **Tomorrow**, **Weekend** (the coming Saturday) and **Next week** (next Monday), each with its day.
- A calendar of one month, weeks from Monday, with today outlined and the chosen day filled. The arrows beside the month, or the arrow keys, move to other months.

Both **Due date** and **Start** can have a time: choosing a day leaves the picker open and moves to the time field at the bottom. Type a time (*9*, *1300*, *14:30*, *2pm*) and press <kbd>Enter</kbd>, or click the field and pick one from the half hours, and the picker closes. For no time, <kbd>Enter</kbd>, **Done**, <kbd>Esc</kbd> or a click outside closes it. A typed date and <kbd>Enter</kbd> close it at once, so *fri 10:00* sets both. Another day chosen keeps the time, and a time set before any day is for today. The **×** beside the time removes it. **Clear** removes the date.

## How the views use them

| View | Due date | Planned date |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Today** | **Overdue** lists open tasks due before today. **Due today** lists tasks due today. | **Planned for today** lists tasks planned for today that aren't overdue or due today. |
| **Upcoming** | Each of the next 14 days lists its deadlines first, sorted by time. **Deadlines only** hides planned tasks and activities; project targets stay. | Tasks planned for the day follow the deadlines. |
| **Calendar**, month | A chip with a flag on the deadline's day, red when overdue. | A chip with a calendar check mark on the planned day. When both dates fall on the same day, the task shows once, as its deadline. |
| **Calendar**, week | The day's header counts its open deadlines, shows the time of a single one, or says how many were missed on a past day. | A task planned at a time is a block at that time; one planned for a day only is in the day's top row, or in a focus block the auto-planner placed from that day on. |
| **Timeline** | The bar ends at the deadline, marked with a flag. | The bar starts on the planned day. |
| **Auto-planner** | Overdue tasks come first, then the earliest deadlines. | A task planned for a later day isn't placed before that day. |

## Planned days don't carry over

A planned date is a single day. When it passes and the task isn't done, the task doesn't move to the next day: without a deadline, it leaves **Today**, the **Today** count in the sidebar and **Upcoming**. It stays on its planned day in the calendar, in its area's or project's list, and on the timeline; when the task is yours, the auto-planner still places it in your next free focus time. Give it a new planned day to bring it back to **Today**.

## Overdue

An open task is overdue once its due date is before today. With a time, its deadline turns red as soon as that time has passed, even on the same day. **Today** lists tasks due on earlier days in the **Overdue** group; a task due earlier today stays in **Due today**, with its time in red.

A task row shows its deadline as a short chip:

| Chip | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **3d overdue** | The deadline was three days ago. |
| **Yesterday** | The deadline was yesterday. |
| **Today** | Due by the end of today. |
| **17:00** | Due today at 17:00; red once that time has passed. |
| **Tomorrow**, **Tomorrow, 10:00** | Due tomorrow. |
| **Sat 26** | Due within the week. |
| **12 Oct** | Due later. |

The details panel adds the distance, such as *in 3 days* or *2 days late*.

## Dates and agent sessions

Dates don't start agent sessions, and neither do [dependencies](/views/timeline#dependencies): a session starts when you start it, or at a request that you allow or that the computer's own settings let start. See [Start a session](/agents/start-and-manage#start-a-session).
