PacedMind works with or without an account. The plan decides where the data lives and where it can be used.

## The two plans

| | One device | Cloud |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Account | None | A PacedMind account, with two-factor sign-in |
| Where the data lives | On this computer, in PacedMind's data folder | In the account |
| Where it can be used | The desktop app on this computer | The desktop app on all your computers, and the web app in the browser |
| Agent sessions | Started on this computer | Started on this computer, or on one of your other computers by asking it |
| Cost | Free | A subscription, paid monthly or yearly, after a 7-day free trial |

Prices depend on the country. They are listed on the [PacedMind website](https://pacedmind.com/#pricing). Paid yearly, a year costs 10 months' worth.

## One device

The desktop app keeps everything on the computer it runs on, and needs no account and no payment. The first time PacedMind opens, it shows the sign-in screen: select **Continue without an account** to use this plan. See [First launch](/getting-started/first-launch) and [Where your data lives](/getting-started/data).

## Cloud

With PacedMind Cloud, the tasks, plans, calendar and sessions are stored in your account, not on the computer. Every computer signed in to the account and the web app show the same data. Images that agents attach to their reports stay on the computer they were saved on.

Every account uses two-factor sign-in: each sign-in asks for a code from an authenticator app, because PacedMind can start agents on your computers. **Add authenticator** in **Settings → Two-factor sign-in** adds a second one as a backup.

Signed in to the desktop app on a computer that kept data without an account, **Settings → Data** offers **Move to account** in the **This computer** row. It works on an account without projects or tasks, replaces the account's areas with the ones from this computer, and leaves a copy on this computer, without its flows.

## The free trial

A new account gets 7 days of Cloud free, counted from sign-up. It needs no card. **Settings → Plan** shows the days left, and in the trial's last three days a banner above every page says when it ends.

Subscribing during the trial keeps the rest of it: the first payment comes when the trial ends. See [Subscribe to Cloud](/accounts-and-cloud/subscribing).

A trial that ends without a subscription leaves the account read-only: everything in it can still be read, deleted or moved to a computer, but not changed. See [When Cloud ends](/accounts-and-cloud/when-cloud-ends).
