Subscribing, switching and canceling all start in **Settings → Plan**. The section is there only when you're signed in to an account, in the desktop app and in the web app.

The section's note says what Cloud gives and that a year costs 10 months' worth. The card is entered on the payment page, never in PacedMind: PacedMind never sees it.

## The Plan row

The **Plan** row says where the account stands:

| The row reads | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| *Free trial, 3 days left (until 2026-10-05)* | The free trial, with the days left and the day it ends. On its last day: *Free trial, ends today*. |
| *Cloud, paid monthly, renews 2026-10-28* | Subscribed, with the day of the next payment. *paid yearly* for a yearly subscription. |
| *Cloud until 2026-10-28, then read-only* | The subscription was canceled. Cloud keeps working until that day. |
| *The last payment didn't go through; the card is tried again* | A payment failed. Cloud keeps working while the card is tried again. |
| *Read-only: subscribe to keep working in Cloud* | The trial or the subscription is over. See [When Cloud ends](/accounts-and-cloud/when-cloud-ends). |
| *Cloud, included with your account* | Cloud without a subscription. The section has nothing else. |

## Subscribe

Before subscribing, during the trial or once Cloud has ended, the section has **Prices for**, **Pay** and **Subscribe**.

1. Open **Settings → Plan**. The **Subscribe** button on the banner goes there too.
2. Under **Prices for**, pick the country whose price to pay. It starts at the country guessed from the time zone of the computer or browser.
3. Under **Pay**, choose **Monthly** or **Yearly**. The price for that country shows next to it, for a month or for a year. The prices are also on the [PacedMind website](https://pacedmind.com/#pricing).
4. Select **Subscribe**. The payment page opens: in the desktop app, in the browser; in the web app, in the same tab, which returns to **Settings** afterwards.
5. Enter the card on the payment page and confirm there.

Once the payment goes through, PacedMind shows the new plan by itself, such as *Cloud, paid monthly, renews 2026-10-28*, also in the desktop app when the payment was made in the browser.

Subscribing during the free trial keeps the rest of it: the first payment comes when the trial ends.

When the payment page can't be opened, PacedMind says *Billing can't be reached right now. Try again in a moment.*

## The Billing row

Once subscribed, the **Billing** row replaces **Prices for** and **Pay**.

### Switch between monthly and yearly

While the subscription runs, the **Billing** row has **Switch to yearly**, or **Switch to monthly** for a yearly subscription. It asks first, such as *Pay yearly from now on? What's left of this period counts toward the new one.*, and then confirms *You pay yearly from now on*. The payment page doesn't open for this, and the subscription keeps the price of the country it was bought in.

The switch isn't offered after canceling, or while a failed payment is tried again.

### Manage billing

**Manage billing** opens the billing page, the same way **Subscribe** opens the payment page. There:

* change the card;
* see the invoices;
* cancel the subscription.

An account that has paid before keeps **Manage billing** after its subscription has ended, for its invoices.

## Cancel

Cancel in **Manage billing**. Cloud stays until the end of the period already paid for, and the **Plan** row reads *Cloud until 2026-10-28, then read-only*, with that period's last day. After it, the account is read-only. See [When Cloud ends](/accounts-and-cloud/when-cloud-ends).

An account whose subscription would renew can't be deleted: cancel first. See [Delete the account](/accounts-and-cloud/when-cloud-ends#delete-the-account).

## When a payment fails

When a payment doesn't go through, a banner above every page says *The last payment for PacedMind Cloud didn't go through. Cloud keeps working while the card is tried again.* Its **Update payment** button opens the billing page, where the card can be changed. The **Plan** row reads *The last payment didn't go through; the card is tried again* in the meantime.

If the card keeps failing, the subscription ends and the account becomes read-only.
