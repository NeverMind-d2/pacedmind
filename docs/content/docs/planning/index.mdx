title: Planning and work hours
description: How deadlines, planned days, priorities, estimates, activities and work hours come together into a plan, and where to see it.
---

PacedMind plans from what the tasks already say. There's no separate schedule to maintain: the auto-planner turns the tasks' dates, priorities and estimates into suggested **focus blocks** inside your work hours, around your calendar activities.

## The inputs

| Input | Role in the plan | Set in |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Due date** | Overdue tasks come first, then the earliest deadlines. | Task details, quick add, calendar, timeline |
| **Planned date** | A task isn't placed before its planned day. | Task details, calendar, timeline |
| **Priority** | Breaks ties; **Urgent** and **High** tasks are planned even without a date. | Task details, quick add |
| **Estimate** | How much time the task gets. | Task details, quick add |
| **Done by** | Only your tasks are planned; agents' tasks get a check block after they finish. | Task details |
| **Activities** | Busy time, kept free with a margin. | Quick add, a click on the activity, agents |
| **Work hours, break and work days** | The time the plan may use. | **Settings → Planning** |

## Where the plan shows

* **[Calendar: week](/views/calendar-week)**: the focus blocks in the grid, and the **Auto-plan** panel with notes, the hours per area and what didn't fit.
* **[Timeline](/views/timeline)**: the **Your time** row, with the planned hours per day.
* **Agents**: `get_agenda` lists each day's activities, deadlines, planned tasks and focus blocks, with the free time left.

## Change the plan

Focus blocks can't be moved by hand; they follow the tasks. To change them:

* move a task to another day: set its **Planned** date, or drag it in the calendar or on the timeline;
* change its priority or its estimate;
* hand it to an agent, or mark it as yours;
* add an activity to block time, or change the work hours.

The week view recomputes the plan every time it loads; **Replan the week** does it on demand.

## Plan with an agent

The **pacedmind-planning** skill plans a day or a week in conversation: it reads the agenda, proposes what to do when, and moves only what you agree to. It also handles requests such as *move X to Friday*, *clear my afternoon* or *I'm sick today*. See [Agent skills](/skills).
