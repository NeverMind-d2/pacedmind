---
title: Work hours and days
description: The focus hours, break and work days that the auto-planner fills, their defaults, and how to change them in Settings or through an agent.
---

The auto-planner only places tasks inside your work hours, on your work days. They're set in **Settings → Planning**, under **Auto-plan**: *The week view fills free focus time between these hours, around your fixed activities.*

| Setting | Meaning | Default |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Focus hours** | When work time starts and ends each work day. | 09:00 to 17:00 |
| **Keep free** | A daily break the planner leaves empty, such as lunch. | 12:30 to 13:30 |
| **Work days** | The days the planner uses. Select a day to switch it on or off. | Mon to Fri |

Changes save when a field loses focus, confirmed by *Saved*, and apply the next time a plan is made.

## Where they show

* The **week view** shades the days that aren't work days and lists the hours under **Rules** in its **Auto-plan** panel. **Change** there opens **Settings → Planning**.
* The **Timeline**'s **Your time** row uses the length of a work day, the focus hours minus the break, as a full bar.
* The **Flows** editor uses the start of the work hours as the default time for **At a set time**: the next work day at that hour.

## Change them through an agent

Agents read the settings with `get_settings` and change them with `update_settings`:

```text
update_settings
  work_start: "08:30"
  work_end: "16:30"
  break_start: "12:00"
  break_end: "12:45"
  work_days: ["mon", "tue", "wed", "thu"]
```

The work hours must start before they end, and so must the break. Times use the 24-hour `HH:mm` format; days are `mon` to `sun`.

## Time outside work hours

Activities outside the work hours still show in the calendar, but they don't reduce the focus time. Tasks can still be planned for any day, including days off; the auto-planner just won't place focus blocks on those days.
