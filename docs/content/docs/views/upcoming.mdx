**Upcoming** covers the next 14 days, from tomorrow on (*Next 14 days*). Today has its own view: see [Today](/views/today).

## Project targets

The first group, **Project targets**, lists every project with a target date, soonest first. Each row shows:

* the project's diamond, name and area;
* its progress: a bar and the done and total tasks, such as *3/5* (*No tasks* when it has none);
* on wide windows, a small 90-day timeline with the target's position;
* the target date and how far away it is, such as *in 12 days*;
* its health.

| Health | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **On track** | Done tasks keep pace with the time passed since the project started, or every task is done. |
| **At risk** | The share of time passed is ahead of the share of tasks done by more than 15 points. |
| **Behind** | Ahead by more than 30 points, or the target date has passed with open tasks. |

Health compares two shares between the project's start date and its target date: the time gone and the tasks done (canceled tasks don't count). A project without a start date is always on track until its target passes. Finished projects drop off the list once their target date has passed.

Select a row to open the project.

## Days

Each of the next 14 days with anything on it gets a group: **Tomorrow**, then the weekday names, with the date and a count. A day lists, in this order:

1. the target dates of projects that end that day (*Q4 planning target date*, with *3 of 5 tasks done*);
2. tasks due that day, sorted by time, then by priority;
3. tasks planned for that day that aren't also due then;
4. the day's calendar activities.

Select a day's name to collapse it. The **+** on a day adds a task planned for that day.

## Everything or deadlines only

The switch in the header chooses what the days show:

* **Everything**: deadlines, planned tasks and activities.
* **Deadlines only**: only tasks due that day. Project targets stay.

With nothing ahead, the view says *Nothing coming up in the next 14 days* (or *No deadlines in the next 14 days*) and reminds that tasks with a due or planned date show up here.
