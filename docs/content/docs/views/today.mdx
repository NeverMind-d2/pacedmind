**Today** is the view PacedMind opens with. Its header shows the date, such as *Thu, 24 Sep*, and it lists everything that needs attention today.

<img src="/docs/screenshots/today.png" alt="The Today view with the Schedule, Overdue, Due today and Planned for today groups" width="1920" height="1200" loading="lazy" />

## Groups

| Group | Contains |
| --- | --- |
| **Schedule** | Today's calendar activities, with their time range and area. The activity in progress is marked **Now**; past ones are faded. |
| **Overdue** | Open tasks due before today, oldest deadline first. Shown in red. |
| **Due today** | Tasks due today: the open ones, and those finished today, crossed out. |
| **Planned for today** | Open tasks planned for today that aren't overdue or due today. |

Within **Due today** and **Planned for today**, tasks sort by priority, then by deadline, with finished tasks last. Empty groups are hidden. With nothing at all, the view says *Nothing is due or planned for today*.

## Filter

The bar under the header switches between:

* **All**: the schedule and the tasks.
* **Tasks**: only the task groups.
* **Activities**: only the schedule.

Its right side counts the tasks: *5 open · 1 done*.

## Add from Today

* **New task** in the header, and the **+** on **Due today** and **Planned for today**, add a task planned for today.
* The **+** on **Schedule** opens quick add in activity mode.

## Using Today

* Click a status icon to mark a task done; click a title to open its details.
* Click an activity in **Schedule** to [change or delete it](/concepts/activities#change-or-remove-an-activity).
* To push a task to another day, open it and change **Start**, or drag it in the [calendar](/views/calendar).
* For a plan of the day with focus times, see the [week view](/views/calendar-week), or ask an agent to plan the day: the **pacedmind-planning** skill reads the agenda and proposes a schedule. See [Agent skills](/skills).
