title: "Calendar: week"
description: The week view lays out activities, your tasks, auto-planned focus blocks and agent sessions hour by hour, from midnight to midnight; drag tasks between hours and days, and see what's left to place.
---

The week view shows one week, Monday to Sunday, from midnight to midnight. It combines the fixed calendar, the tasks you put at a time, the focus blocks the auto-planner suggests for the rest, and the agent sessions that ran, and lets you drag tasks to the hour and day you mean to work on them.

Open it with **Week** in the calendar's header, by selecting a day number in the month view, or with **Go to Week** in the command menu.

## The header

* **Calendar**, the dates and the ISO week number, such as *21 to 27 Sep · week 39*.
* The arrows (**Previous week**, **Next week**) and **Today**, which returns to the current week.
* **Month** and **Week**, to switch views.

The bar under the header is the legend, and states the week's load: *22.5 h planned of 26 h focus time left* (for the current week) or *… of … h focus time* (for a later week).

| Legend | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Fixed** | Calendar activities. |
| **Yours** | Tasks you put at a time, as long as their estimate. |
| **Auto-planned** | Focus blocks the auto-planner suggests for your other tasks, such as *WRK-31 Prepare slides for Q4 planning*, with a dashed outline. |
| **Check a finished session** | A 45-minute block to review work an agent finished, such as *Check DEV-21*. It has a terminal icon and a navy outline. |
| **Claude Code**, **Codex** | Agent sessions, as blocks from when they started to when they ended, lightly tinted in the agent's color and marked with its icon. A running session's block grows until it ends and has a pulsing dot. Tasks handed to an agent that you put at a time have the same tint. |

## The grid

* Each day's header shows its name and date, with today's date in navy. A flag shows the day's open deadlines: the time of a single deadline that has one, otherwise their count, or *2 missed* on a past day, in red when late.
* The hours run from 00:00 to 24:00. The view opens a little before the current time in the current week, and just before the work hours in other weeks; scroll for the rest of the day.
* Days outside the work days are shaded, and so are the hours outside the work hours.
* The **Day** row under the headers holds the tasks planned for a day without a time. It shows while a day has one, or while you drag a task.
* A navy line marks the current time.
* Overlapping blocks sit side by side. Blocks in the past are faded.
* Hover a block for its title and time. Select a task's block, a check block or a session to open the task's details, and a fixed block to open its activity, to [change or delete it](/concepts/activities#change-or-remove-an-activity).

## Drag tasks

Drag with the mouse; blocks land on the quarter hour, and a dashed outline shows where the task will go. <kbd>Esc</kbd> cancels a drag. On a phone, a tap opens the task.

* **Onto an hour**: the task is planned for that day at that time, as long as its estimate, such as *DEV-22 is planned for Thu, 1 Oct at 10:15*. The auto-planner leaves it there and plans your other tasks around it.
* **An auto-planned block**: dragging it puts its task at the time you drop it, and it becomes yours.
* **The bottom edge** of one of your blocks: stretches or shortens it, which changes the task's estimate.
* **Onto the Day row**: the task keeps the day and loses its time, so the auto-planner places it again.
* **Onto the Tasks list**: the task loses its planned day (*DEV-22 isn't planned for a day now*).

Activities, check blocks and agent sessions don't move.

## The Tasks list

**Tasks**, in the panel on the right, lists the open tasks that aren't on this week yet, most urgent first: the ones without a planned day, and the ones whose planned day passed (with that day in red). Each shows its key, title and estimate; tasks handed to Claude Code or Codex carry the agent's tint. **Filter tasks** narrows the list by key or title. Drag one onto an hour or onto a day's row to plan it; select it to open its details.

## Add a task on a day

Click the free space in a day's column, or the day's header (*New task on Wed, 30 Sep*), to open [quick add](/tasks/quick-add) with the task planned for that day. While the pointer is over a day's column, the day's header lights up and shows a **+** in place of its deadline flag. On a phone, tap the free space of the day shown.

A new task gets only the day, so the half hour clicked doesn't matter for it: the [auto-planner](/planning/auto-planner) places it in free focus time from its planned day on, until you drag it to an hour. Switched to **Activity**, the dialog starts the activity at the half hour that was clicked, such as *Wed 30 Sep, 16:00*. A click on a fixed activity opens the activity instead, and a click on a header's deadline flag doesn't add anything.

## The Auto-plan panel

**Auto-plan**, next to **Tasks** in the panel on the right, explains the plan. The panel hides while a task's details are open.

* **Show auto-planned blocks** hides and shows the focus blocks. When off, the panel says *Auto-plan is off. Only fixed events, your own blocks and agent sessions are shown.*
* The status line: *Planned at 14:05 · 6 tasks in 9 blocks*, or *Planned at 14:05 · nothing to place this week*. For a past week: *This week is over, so there is nothing left to plan.*
* Up to four notes on what the planner did, such as *DEV-21 finished, so 45 min to check it was added today at 15:00*, *WRK-27 is overdue, planned today at 09:00*, or *Split WRK-31 into 2 blocks*.
* **Rest of this week** (or **This week**): planned hours of the available focus hours, with a bar split by area color and the hours per area.
* **Didn't fit this week**: tasks that need more time than is free, such as *WRK-36 Send the Q4 plan to the team needs 2 h and there's no free slot left this week.* Select one to open it.
* **Rules**: the work hours and break from **Settings → Planning**, and the planner's fixed rules, with **Change** to open that page.
* **Replan the week** reloads the week and plans it again from the current time.

The plan is recomputed every time the view loads and after every drag. Focus blocks aren't stored: they change when the tasks change, and a dragged one becomes a task at a time. See [The auto-planner](/planning/auto-planner).

## Weeks ahead

The planner fills the days from now on in order, so a later week only gets what the weeks before it leave over. A later week states its full focus time. Its **Didn't fit this week** list names only tasks meant for that week: those planned before its end, or, without a planned day, with no deadline or a deadline before its end.
