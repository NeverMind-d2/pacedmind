title: Inbox

The **Inbox** lists open tasks without an area and without a project: *Tasks without an area or project*. It's the place to capture a task quickly and decide later where it belongs.

## What it shows

* Only open tasks. Done and canceled tasks leave the Inbox.
* Grouped by status: **In review**, **In progress**, **Todo**, **Backlog**. Within a group, the most urgent priority comes first.
* The sidebar shows how many open tasks the Inbox holds.

An empty Inbox says *Your inbox is clear*.

## Add to the Inbox

Tasks land in the Inbox when they're created without an area or project:

* In quick add, leave the area chip on **Inbox** and the project chip empty (**Project**).
* Agents create Inbox tasks with `create_task` when they leave out both `project` and `area`.

Inbox tasks get **INB** keys, such as INB-3. The key stays when the task moves to an area later.

## Sort the Inbox

Open a task and give it a place in the details panel:

* **Project**: puts the task in a project and in the project's area.
* **Area**: puts it directly in an area, without a project.

The task then leaves the Inbox. While sorting, it's a good moment to set a priority, a planned day or a due date, and an estimate, or to cancel what won't be done.

An agent can triage the Inbox too: the **pacedmind-review** skill goes through it in small batches and suggests a place and dates for each task. See [Agent skills](/skills).
