title: "Calendar: month"
description: The month calendar shows deadlines, planned days, activities and project targets; click a day to add a task on it, move tasks between days by dragging, and plan tasks that have no date from the No date panel.
---

**Calendar** opens on the current month. It shows every task with a date, the calendar activities and the projects' target dates, and lets tasks move between days by drag and drop.

<img src="/docs/screenshots/calendar-month.png" alt="The month calendar with deadlines, activities and planned tasks, and the No date panel on the right" width="1920" height="1200" loading="lazy" />

## The header

* The month and year, such as *September 2026*.
* The arrows (**Previous month**, **Next month**) and **Today**, which returns to the current month.
* **Month** and **Week**, which switch between this view and the [week view](/views/calendar-week).

The bar under the header has four filters, each shown or hidden with a click:

| Filter | Shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Deadlines** | Tasks on their due date, with a flag. |
| **Activities** | Calendar activities, with the area's dot and the start time. |
| **Planned** | Tasks on their planned day, with a calendar check mark. |
| **Project targets** | Projects on their target date, with a diamond. |

Its right side summarizes the month's deadlines: *3 deadlines left this month* for the current month, *No deadlines in October* for a later one, and for a past month either *2 overdue from August*, in red, or *Nothing left open from August*.

## The grid

The grid runs from Monday to Sunday and shows five or six weeks. Days outside the month are shaded, and today's number is navy. Select a day's number to open that week in the week view (**Open this week**).

Each day lists its items in this order: deadlines, project targets, planned tasks, activities, then finished tasks, crossed out. Deadlines on today are highlighted and overdue deadlines are red. A task whose planned day is its due day shows once, as its deadline. Canceled tasks don't show.

Click an activity to open it, to [change or delete it](/concepts/activities#change-or-remove-an-activity).

When a day holds more than fits, **+3 more** opens the whole day in a small panel; <kbd>Esc</kbd> or its **×** closes it.

## Add a task on a day

Click a day's free space, or the **+** that appears in its corner while the pointer is over the day (*New task on Wed, 30 Sep*), to open [quick add](/tasks/quick-add) with the task planned for that day, such as *Planned Wed 30 Sep*. The panel that **+3 more** opens has the same **+** next to its **×**. On a phone, tap the day's free space.

A click on a task, an activity or a project target doesn't add a task, and neither does a click that only closes an open menu or panel. A time typed in the title without a date, such as *15:00*, is on the clicked day too.

## The task menu

Click a task in the grid, or focus it and press <kbd>Enter</kbd> or <kbd>Space</kbd>, to open its menu. The menu names the task and offers:

| Item | Result |
| --- | --- |
| **Open task** | Opens the details panel. |
| **Change date** | Opens quick choices: **Today**, **Tomorrow**, **In a week**, or a date (and, for a deadline, a time) with **Set**. It moves the date the chip stands for: the due date for a deadline, the planned date for a planned task. |
| **Plan for a day** | For tasks from the **No date** panel: gives the task a planned day. |
| **Remove due date** or **Remove planned date** | Clears the date the chip stands for. |

A task planned for the same day as its deadline shows as one deadline chip, and that chip stands for both dates: **Change date** moves both, and **Remove due date** clears both.
| **Delete task** | Asks *Delete WRK-12? Click again*; the second click deletes the task. |

Finished tasks offer only **Open task** and **Delete task**.

## Drag and drop

Drag a task to another day to move it:

* **A deadline** moves its due date to that day and keeps its time.
* **A planned task** moves its planned day.
* **A deadline that is also the planned day** moves both dates together.

While a planned task, or a task from the **No date** panel, hovers over a day, a **Due** flag appears in the day's corner (*Drop here to make it the due date*). Dropping it on the flag makes that day the task's deadline. A planned task dropped there also stays planned for that day.

Each move is confirmed with a message, such as *WRK-12 is now due Fri, 2 Oct* or *Planned WRK-12 for Fri, 2 Oct*. Finished tasks can't be dragged.

## The No date panel

The **No date** panel on the right lists open tasks that have neither a due date nor a planned date, with their count, the most active and urgent first (up to 15, then *and 4 more*). Its hint reads *Drag a task onto a day to plan it. Drop it on the deadline flag to set a due date instead.*

* Drag a task from the panel onto a day to plan it, or onto the **Due** flag to give it a deadline.
* Drag a dated task onto the panel to remove the date it was dragged by (both dates, for a deadline chip that is also the planned day). While dragging, the panel shows *Drop here to remove the date*. A task that loses its last date says *WRK-12 has no date now*.

The panel hides when every open task has a date and while a task's details are open. It opens again during a drag, to take a task.
